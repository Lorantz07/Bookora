About Bookora / A reading room

Built for slow reading.

Bookora is a small, independent reading room: public-domain books, presented with care, and a catalogue open to browse without an account. The catalogue is searchable and introduced edition by edition, and six complete texts now open in a measured browser reader.

Stage 3 / Reader Available now

A measured page, with your place kept.

Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, The Yellow Wallpaper, and The Time Machine open in the reader. Pages re-measure when you change the type, and your progress stays in this browser. The catalogue remains local; no external APIs are called.

01

Sources

Texts are public-domain editions from Project Gutenberg, Indonesian Wikisource, and Malay Wikisource. The three reader texts are bundled with the site; source links and licence notes stay recorded in the local catalogue data and appear on every edition page.

02

Privacy

Bookora does not ask for an account and does not build reading profiles. Reading progress and display preferences stay in your browser's local storage, and clearing site data removes them. No analytics or advertising scripts are included.

03

Copyright

The book covers on this site are original Bookora illustrations, not reproductions of publisher artwork. Public-domain texts remain in the public domain; transcription and translation credits are shown on each edition where known.
